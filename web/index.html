How it works

Your ROM stays with you

Apply a site translation with its original ROM, or your own UPS, IPS, BPS, xdelta, APS or PPF patch. Files stay in your browser. Translation releases check the ROM and result hashes. UPS patches also work with RomPatcher.js.
