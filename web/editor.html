Verifying the ROM and any patches…

With your ROM

The English of every line appears beside its translation, drawn with the exact game glyphs and measured against its real window. Pictures with text show the original sheet from your file.

Without a ROM

The editor shows the keys and the translations with their states; the English, the search by it and the checks that need it wait for the ROM. Glyphs the toolkit does not ship are drawn as stand-ins with a fallback width. You can add the ROM later from the top bar.

What you can do

  • Translate texts, with line and page breaks placed by key or button.
  • Edit glossary terms and see every line they affect.
  • Redraw the text pixels of pictures over the original sheet.
  • Check the Pokédex names in your language.

How changes land

  • Drafts stay in this browser until you export them.
  • Export them as JSON for review; every change is checked and reviewed before a release.
  • With gen3tl web server start --page editor running on your machine, text drafts save into the catalog.

      Download the original sheet (an indexed PNG with the ROM palette), change only the text pixels and keep the palette, then upload it with its text areas.       Tile sheet

      The sheet as the build writes it: the ROM's tiles, then one tile for each letter the pack draws, framed. gen3tl lang sprite-sheet renders the same image from your ROM.

      Glyphs

      Each cell shows the glyph as the translation installs it, its character, code, tile number and width; a red line marks the width. Marked cells come from the language pack; with your ROM loaded, a glyph the pack redraws shows the original of your ROM beside it. Click a glyph the pack draws to redraw it; the redraw stays in this browser until you export or submit it.

      Screen preview

      The key window as the naming screen prints it: each row of the page at its pixel position. Colours approximate the game's palette; glyphs come from the language pack and, for the base font, from your ROM.

      Keys

      Each key: the glyph printed at its place, the character it enters with its code, the x position and the 8-pixel tile in the key window, and where the font takes the glyph from. Dimmed keys lie outside the columns the cursor reaches; a glyph outside the cursor frame of its column is marked. Select a key of the language pack to change the character it enters: its glyph is printed in the middle of its cursor frame.

      Page label

      The name of the page on the page-swap button, as the game draws it: the project's translated graphic, else the ROM's. The button shows it while the page before it is open.

      Page order

        Select moves down this list; from the last page it returns to the top. The arrows move a page; keyboard changes stay in this browser and travel in Export.
